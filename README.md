🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis.

📌 Project Overview.
This project performs Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis using Python, SQL, and Power BI.
The goal is to understand customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, and business trends to support data-driven decisions.


🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer demographics and behavior
Identify top-selling products and categories
Study payment method preferences
Understand seasonal purchasing trends
Generate business insights for decision-making.

📂 Dataset Information
Source: Customer Shopping Behavior Dataset
Format: CSV
Records: Customer purchase transactions
Key Features:
Customer ID
Age
Gender
Category
Purchase Amount
Payment Method
Location
Frequency of Purchases.
